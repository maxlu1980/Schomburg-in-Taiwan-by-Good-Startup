## V87 — a real train, real stairs, announcements, the dog on the floor, the view under the hand, Köln nearer to Köln
- **The trains rebuilt** (`src/koeln/city/k3d_g.js`): every car is a shell lofted through its cross-sections at its true size, with the livery, the glazing and the doors laid on the shell's own surface. The fast set's nose had been half a sphere stretched to the body's whole height — a disc taller than the car; it is now a long nose drawn down to a low point over a dark valance, with a raked windscreen, cab windows and lamps (white at the head, red at the tail). The fast set is **this game's own design in its own colours** (pearl white, a midnight window band, cobalt doors and waist line) — not a copy of any railway's train, and it carries no railway's logo. Wheels stand on the rails (1.435 m), bogies under the skirts, pantographs on insulators. The trains have their own satin paint (the engine's glossy paint mirrored the open sky under the hall's roof and washed every colour pale).
- **The station's stairs to the rules for a station's stairs** (`k3d_hbf.js`, `HSTR` in `k3d_a.js`): two flights of 18 risers of 16.2 cm with 30 cm treads and a 1.8 m landing (they were 18 blocks of 32 cm between tiled slabs 4.7 m tall); every tread's edge marked, the first and last of each flight in white; granite-clad strings that rise with the flights under a glass panel, steel handrails on both sides from before the first riser to beyond the last; the upper flight boxed in to the ceiling with the departure and arrival sheets on its end wall; ribbed white fields of attention at foot and head; a kerb, posts, glass and a rail round the well on the platform. The walking surface, the camera's solids, the wells, the links between the levels and the people's ways follow the new shape.
- **The platforms**: grey concrete slabs with a light granite edge unit, the white edge line and the white ribbed guiding strip; steel-mesh benches and bins; a cable tray under the roof with lamps, loudspeakers and the blue cubes of the sections A–G; the hall's steel dark and matte; the hall's ends glazed in small panes; **an indicator over each track** showing the game's own train for it (time, service, destination, way; "Zug fährt ein", "Bitte einsteigen").
- **Announcements in the station** (`src/koeln/isle/k_ann.js`, generated by `game/tts/make_ann.py`): a two-tone chime and a German announcement when one of the game's trains runs in and before it leaves, an English one for the long-distance trains, a welcome and general notices; through a loudspeaker's voice band with the hall's echo (duller down in the passage); the words on the screen with what they mean in the page's language; only inside the station, silent when the sound is off; the plate of words makes way while one of her own lines, a talk card or a panel is on the screen, and on a low window shows the meaning in the page's language alone. **Our own wording in the usual order of a platform announcement, spoken by a synthetic voice (Supertonic 3) — not a railway's recordings.**
- **The dog rests on the floor** (`game/tools/patchdog.py` → `src/koeln/lib/DOG.js`): lying, the elbows were driven 7.6 cm under the ground (the forepaws were set far ahead of the shoulders); the lying pose is now elbows back, forearms flat, chest and belly on the floor. Sitting, the croup had hung 8 cm over the floor (a squat): the rump is now on the ground, the chest upright over straight forelegs, the hind paws under it. For every kind and every rest pose the lowest point of body and legs is measured once the pose has settled and the hip is raised by whatever lies under the floor (`ktest/dog.html` lists all 40 pose/kind pairs: none under the floor now).
- **Everyone stands on the floor that is seen** (`k3d_e.js`, `k3d_dom.js`): the plazas' granite, the old town's setts, Hohe Straße's paving, the lawns, the cathedral's floor slabs and the bar's tiles are drawn 5–14 mm over the pavement or plinth under them, and walkers stood on the layer below — soles and paws that much inside the slab, a sitting dog's haunches with them. Each layer is now also the floor walked on. `qa/feet.js` (`npm run audit:feet`) puts her at every named place and compares the height she stands at with the surface drawn under her.
- **The view under the hand on every device** (`src/koeln/isle/k_ctl.js`, hooks in `game/tools/kpatch_v87.py`): a mouse drags the view with the left or the right button and zooms with the wheel (a click still walks there); on a touch screen the left side is the stick and the right side turns the view — both thumbs at once — and two fingers pinch; keys `,` `.` turn, `PgUp` `PgDn` tilt, `+` `−` zoom, `C` puts the camera behind her; a gamepad's sticks walk and look. The camera goes on fitting the place and her movement (zones, walls, stairs) and now also draws back a little when she runs.
- **On a computer the keys are shown where the buttons are**: a pad for the view with the key under each button, the walking keys W A S D and Shift as buttons, the actions' buttons (car, jump, punch, overclock, special, talk) clickable with their keys, the keys on the top bar's panels; a hint for the pointer in use; and the help card (H) shows a keyboard with every key in its place, the mouse, the touch screen and the gamepad. With a finger: three view buttons (turn left, turn right, behind her). The key caps are drawn by the style sheet from an attribute (`data-k` / `data-kc`) and the buttons carry `aria-keyshortcuts`: written into the buttons as text they made the visible text differ from the accessible name (WCAG 2.5.3, found by axe), and the game rewrites the insides of its own buttons while playing.
- **A phone held sideways**: the actions' column (five buttons from 166 px up) was taller than the screen and ran over the top bar; on a low, wide touch screen the actions now stand in two rows under the thumb (strike, jump, car below; overclock and special above), the phone button beside them, and the announcement's plate lies between the round map and the actions.
- **The cathedral inside**: the stone smooth and pale (it read as a chequer of blocks), dark oak pews, stone figures on the nave's piers, the aisles' windows repainted in five registers (canopy, great figure, writing, small scenes under arcades, shields).
- **Roncalliplatz**: the Roman-Germanic Museum as it stands — a pale box clad in light slabs over a glazed ground floor set back behind slim piers, its name in red letters towards the cathedral (it was a box of raw concrete with a dark plate).
- Checks added: `qa/views.js` (reference views), `qa/feet.js` (feet on the floor), `qa/scenarios/ctl_desk.json`, `ctl_touch.json`, `dog_rest.json`. The scenario runner's steps are no longer dropped by the frame cap (`GFX.lastF = 0` before each). Render load against V86 at six places (mid tier): draw calls −8…+2 %, triangles 0…+10 %.

## V85 — steady floors, the camera between levels, the opening score played by a sampled orchestra
- **Floor flicker fixed everywhere**: a coplanar audit (`qa/coplanar.js`) found the pavement lying a few millimetres under the setts, the plazas, the lawns and the tree pits, and at exactly the height of the station's passages and entrance hall (the pavement's slabs broke through the station's tiles in saw-teeth). The ground's layers are now drawn in a fixed order of depth (polygon offset per layer, in depth-buffer steps, so it holds at any distance and angle); the station's floors sit 2 cm over the street; the station's and the viaducts' ballast no longer overlap; the board tiles' icons lie clear of their discs. `qa/flicker.js` + `qa/flicker_diff.py` measure it (pixels that change when the camera moves 1 mm).
- **No more view blocked between the passages and the platforms**: the stairs' wells were closed by an up-facing sheet at the ceiling's height, the track bed and the platforms' concrete bodies; the camera was held under the deck behind a climbing walker (the arm closed onto her back, the view went into the slab). The wells are open now, the camera may pass through them and rises and sinks with her (the higher angles first on a climb), the flights are solid to it, and passers-by or the dog who come between the camera and Mira step out of the picture. `qa/scenarios/stairs_up.json` / `stairs_down.json`.
- **The opening film's music played by a sampled orchestra** (`game/tools/mkscore85.py` → `src/koeln/score85.js`): the same arrangement plan and clock as V81, written for the sections (first and second violins, violas, cellos and basses with legato, short, pizzicato and tremolo articulations; flutes, oboes, clarinets, bassoons; horns, trumpets, trombones, tuba; timpani, harp, glockenspiel, concert bass drum and cymbals), shaped by bow/breath dynamics, rendered with FluidSynth and the MuseScore General soundfont (VSCO 2 CE string sections, CC0; MIT), mixed in a synthesised concert hall and mastered to −16 LUFS (MP3, 0.9 MB). The synthesised score of V81 remains as the fallback.
- **Bookmark name**: on the published site (GitHub Pages) and in the standalone file the page is called "Schomburg in Taiwan by Good Startup" in tabs and bookmarks (`application-name`; the hosted artifact keeps its per-guide titles).
- CI runs on `ubuntu-24.04` (Playwright 1.56 does not support Ubuntu 26.04, the new `ubuntu-latest`).
