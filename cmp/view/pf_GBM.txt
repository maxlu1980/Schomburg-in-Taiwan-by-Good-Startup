WHY:
  - One resin for priming, scratch coats, mortars (5–30 mm) and epoxy screeds (11–150 mm): fewer products on site, one mixing rule (1 : 1 with sand for the scratch coat, approx. 3 : 25 for the screed).
  - Published tensile adhesion ≥ 1.5 N/mm² on concrete that is dry to matt damp; the comparable primers state a dry substrate (≤ 4 % moisture, no rising damp) as the condition.
  - Flexural strength approx. 35 N/mm² (DIN EN 196-1) against approx. 15 N/mm² published for the Sikafloor-156 mortar — the figures are not from identical test bodies and are quoted as printed.
  - Pot life approx. 35 minutes at a simple 100 : 50 mixing ratio.
