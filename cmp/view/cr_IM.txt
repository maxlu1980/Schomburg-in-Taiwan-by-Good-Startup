WHY:
  - A crystalline mortar: the active ingredients grow into the capillaries of the concrete around the chase and the cove — the comparable products are plugging binders (Lamposilex, SikaSet Waterplug) or a rigid slurry (SikaTop Seal-107), not crystalline.
  - Working time approx. 30 minutes for the chase and the cove in one pass; the rapid plugs set within 1–3 minutes and are meant for the running leak only (in the SCHOMBURG system FIX-20-T takes that role).
  - Published consumption per metre of chase and cove (approx. 1.3 kg/m) — the quantity follows the drawing.
  - AQUAFIN-IC follows wet in wet after 1–3 hours: one crystalline system from the crack to the surface.
