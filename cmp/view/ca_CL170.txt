WHY:
  - Two effects from one liquid: the crystalline waterproofing and a plasticising effect that reduces the water demand of the mix — the comparable liquids are pore blockers or crystalline admixtures without a stated plasticising action.
  - Minimum cement content 270 kg/m³ (CEM I), published; Sika WT-100 L requires 350 kg/m³ and a w/c ≤ 0.45.
  - Dosed with the mixing water at the plant or into the truck on site, with the mixing time per m³ stated — the procedure is written for the Taiwanese ready-mix practice (truck dosing).
  - Minimum binder contents are published per cement type (CEM I / II / III), so the recipe check is part of the data sheet.
