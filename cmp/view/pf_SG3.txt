WHY:
  - A two-component resin primer — no cement component to batch (Sikafloor-81 EpoCem and Triblock P are three-component epoxy-cement systems), mixed in one step at 100 : 52.
  - Published as watertight against negative pressing water up to 3 bar; the comparable products are described for damp or green concrete without a stated pressure rating.
  - Overcoating window of 12 hours to 5 days gives the schedule room; the following resin layers bond to the sanded second coat.
  - The same resin serves as scratch coat and mortar binder (1 : 1 and approx. 3 : 25 with quartz sand).
