WHY:
  - A true two-component, non-foaming elastic resin (1 : 1 by volume): the seal is a solid, soft-elastic body of resin in the crack — the single-component competitors (SikaInject-232, Nitofill PU150) foam with water and seal with a foam.
  - Shore A approx. 47 after 21 hours and final curing in approx. 5 hours at +20 °C: the component can be loaded the same day.
  - Viscosity approx. 125 mPa·s reaches the fine branches of the crack; comparable in class to Sika Injection-201 CE (~100 mPa·s) and well below the 300–650 cps of the single-component resins.
  - Consumption approx. 1.1 kg per litre of voids is published, so the quantity can be estimated from the crack volume.
