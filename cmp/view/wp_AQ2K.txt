WHY:
  - Elongation approx. 192 % (ASTM D412) against approx. 40 % published for SikaTop-588 Seal on the same method: the slurry stretches with the crack instead of tearing.
  - Crack bridging published on three methods (EN 14891 > 0.75 mm, PG MDS/FPD 0.4 mm, ASTM C836 > 2.6 mm) — the figures are comparable method by method; Vandex BB75E-Z publishes < 0.8 mm, Sikalastic-152 ~1.2 mm (EN 1062-7).
  - Both pressure directions published: 2.5 bar positive and 0.75 bar negative (WTA 4-6); the comparison products publish either a positive value or none.
  - CO₂ diffusion Sd > 200 m at 2 mm: protects the reinforcement against carbonation as well as the structure against water.
