WHY:
  - An elastic epoxy (Shore A approx. 80) for the sealing of tanks, channels and pits — it follows the small movements of the concrete; the comparable epoxy coatings are rigid (Shore D 80–82).
  - The published chemical-resistance list names the media of the wastewater plant by name (acids, alkalis, fuels, oils, solvents, formaldehyde); Sikafloor-381 and Mapecoat I 24 refer to lists on request.
  - Pot life 45–55 minutes at +20 °C with a 7 : 1 ratio — the longest working time of the four.
  - Applied directly on blasted steel without a primer; on concrete over ASODUR-SG3-thix.
