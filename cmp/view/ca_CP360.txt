WHY:
  - Crystalline and hydrophobic in one powder: the pores are blocked by crystal growth and made water-repellent, two mechanisms against capillary water.
  - Dosage approx. 0.75–1.25 % of the cement — in water-soluble bags for the mixer truck, with the mixing time per m³ stated.
  - Minimum cement content from 270 kg/m³ (CEM I) is published; Sika WT-200 P and Idrocrete KR 1000 require 350 kg/m³.
  - The data sheet names the CEM types and blends for which the dosage applies; the comparable sheets give a general dosage per m³ or per 100 kg of cementitious material.
