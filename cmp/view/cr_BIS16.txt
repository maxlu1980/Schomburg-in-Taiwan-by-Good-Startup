WHY:
  - Compressive strength approx. 40 N/mm² at 28 days — the highest of the published values of the fine mortars compared (20–34 N/mm²).
  - Up to 6 mm in one step on walls and floors; Sika MonoTop-3020 and Planitop 200 stop at 5 and 3 mm per coat.
  - Pot life approx. 60 minutes for large areas of fine smoothing.
  - Classified to EN 1504-3 (R2) like the repair mortar it closes — one standard across the system; Planitop 200 is classified as an EN 1504-2 coating.
