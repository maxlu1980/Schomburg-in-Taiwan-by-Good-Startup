WHY:
  - One bag for three jobs — corrosion protection of the reinforcement, bond coat and contact slurry — with the water addition as the only variable; SikaTop Armatec-110 EpoCem is a three-component epoxy-cement kit, Mapefer 1K Zero a rebar coating without a stated bond-coat consumption.
  - One-component and cement-based: brushed into the matt damp substrate, the repair mortar follows wet in wet.
  - Published consumption per mm for both uses (approx. 1.5 and 1.6 kg/m²), so the quantities of the system can be calculated.
  - Flexible (polymer-modified) — the system is designed for the ASOCRET-BIS mortars on top; the values of the comparable products are quoted as published.
