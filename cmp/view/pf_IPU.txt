WHY:
  - Shore A approx. 40 with 25 % permissible deformation: firm enough for the wheels of forklifts and trolleys, elastic enough for the field and wall joints of the slab.
  - Service temperature −40 to +80 °C, published; Sikaflex PRO-3 is published to +70 °C.
  - Tear resistance approx. 9.0 N/mm² and recovery > 90 % — the joint returns after the load.
  - Edges chamfered under vehicle traffic and a width : depth of 1 : 0.8 are specified in the technical data sheet, so the detail is designed, not improvised.
