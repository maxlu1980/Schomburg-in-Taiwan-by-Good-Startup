WHY:
  - One component, no accelerator to dose: the resin reacts with the water in the crack itself — SikaInject-233 and Nitofill PU130 need 2–10 % accelerator for their reaction times, Sika Injection-101 RC is a two-component resin.
  - Foams to up to approx. 40 times its volume, at the top of the range published by the comparable products (20–45×).
  - Designed as the first step of the two-step injection: the foam stops the water, AQUAFIN-P4 then seals the crack permanently through the same packers.
