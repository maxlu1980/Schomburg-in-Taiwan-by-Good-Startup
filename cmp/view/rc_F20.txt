WHY:
  - Swellable: the plug expands as it sets and locks into the dovetailed chase against the running water.
  - Strength development published at 1 hour, 24 hours and 28 days (approx. 3 / 15 / 30 N/mm²) and consumption per litre of void (approx. 2 kg/l) — the comparable plugs publish strength at 7 days or in psi, and no yield per void.
  - Working time approx. 2 minutes: long enough to press the plug and hold it, short enough to stop the flow.
  - Part of the crystalline system: the plug is overcoated with ASOCRET-IM and AQUAFIN-IC, no separate waterstop product line.
