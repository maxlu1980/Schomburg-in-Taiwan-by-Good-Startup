WHY:
  - Classified as an OS 5b surface protection system (Rili-SIB / DIN V 18026) — the German class for crack-bridging protection of concrete; none of the comparison sheets claims the class.
  - Dynamic crack bridging class B2 at −20 °C after 2,000 hours of UV weathering is published — the behaviour in winter and after ageing, not only the static value at +20 °C.
  - CO₂ diffusion Sd > 200 m against 104 m (Sikalastic-6100 FX) and > 50 m (SikaTop-550 Seal): a carbonation brake of twice the thickness-equivalent.
  - Consumption for the OS 5b build-up is published as a system value (approx. 5.5 kg/m²).
